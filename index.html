2025 to 2026 · Robotics course, EMINES

RecyBot, a robot that recycles plastic bottles

RecyBot is a recycling station built by our team. You insert a plastic bottle, an AI model checks that it is an empty PET bottle with no cap, and the machine shreds it into flakes that are collected for recycling.

I worked in the computer vision team, and I was in charge of the brand: the cover of the machine, the marketing video and the website, which I built myself.

The cover talks to the person standing in front of the machine. The front explains how it works in four steps. One side gives three facts about plastic in Morocco and the other gives the safety rules. The back has a QR code that opens the website.

June to September 2026 · Lisbon, Portugal

A permit dashboard for EPAL

The team was reading more than 400 permits for wastewater treatment plants by hand, and each one took 45 minutes or more. With another intern, I built a tool that reads a permit and shows the key information on a dashboard in under a minute.

The dashboard shows at a glance which permits are valid, which have expired and which expire in the next 8 months. I pitched it to the board and they adopted it. Some departments preferred the manual method at first, and I won them over too.

NeuraTech

A project to give people with severe paralysis a way to communicate again, without surgery.

I interviewed potential users at the KHOTWA Rehabilitation Center in Ben Guerir and compared 4 existing solutions, then built the value proposition and the business model. I pitched it to investors at Gitex Africa, and it was selected for the MIT Sandbox incubation program. It also won the Best Pitch Award at AgorAI.

January 2025 · Mines Paris - PSL × OCP Chair

Water for Moroccan industry

Moroccan industry uses about 1.03 billion m³ of water a year. In a team of 12, we compared recycling, industrial symbiosis and desalination with international examples such as Kalundborg in Denmark.

We found a potential reduction of 68% and recommended a closed-loop water scenario for the Aïn Sebaâ industrial zone.
