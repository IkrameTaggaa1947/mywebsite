June to September 2026 · Lisbon, Portugal

Digitizing permit reviews at EPAL

EPAL's team reviewed 413 wastewater discharge permits by hand. Each permit sets the conditions treated water must meet before it is released into the environment, and each review took 45 minutes or more. With another intern, I built a Python pipeline and a Power BI dashboard that cut the review to about 50 seconds per permit.

The dashboard shows at a glance which permits are valid, which have expired and which expire in the next 8 months. I presented it to the board, which adopted it, then helped other departments, reluctant at first, make the switch.

January 2025 · Mines Paris – PSL × OCP Chair

Water for Moroccan industry

Moroccan industry used about 1.54 billion m³ of water in 2024. In a team of 12, we compared recycling, industrial symbiosis and desalination with international examples such as Kalundborg in Denmark.

We estimated that industry could save about 1.03 billion m³ of water, a 68% cut in its need for conventional fresh water, and recommended a closed-loop water scenario for the Aïn Sebaâ industrial zone.

November 2024 · Client project for OCP S.A.

Digitizing OCP's monthly accounting close

Transformation

We interviewed OCP's Finance and Management Control department to understand how 10 teams run the monthly close, then wrote the specifications for a tool to track it.

In a team of four, we built the web platform: live progress tracking, task dependencies, deadline reminders, and an alert if the month is closed before every task is done.
